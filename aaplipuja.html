No last-minute cancellations, missing samagri, or vague pricing. AapliPuja brings total clarity to your sacred ceremonies.

What Pooja am I booking?

Clear ceremony descriptions, traditional significance, and duration details for Griha Pravesh, Satyanarayan, Ganesh Pooja, and more.

What is included?

Standardized packages ranging from Guruji services only to complete all-inclusive Pooja kits with essential Samagri.

What Samagri is required?

Comprehensive checklist of items provided by us vs. fresh items (flowers, fruits, sweets) needed from your home.

When will it happen?

Interactive Muhurat selector based on traditional Panchang calendars so you select exact auspice time slots.

Who will perform it?

Background-verified, experienced Gurujis fluent in Marathi, Hindi, or English assigned directly to your booking.

How much will I pay?

100% upfront, transparent pricing with no hidden charges or unexpected demands on ceremony day.
