SJ Coders AI Scribe

Turn Clinical Conversations into Structured Documentation

AI Scribe helps healthcare professionals reduce documentation effort by transforming ambient clinical conversations into structured clinical notes that clinicians can review, edit, approve, and securely send to their EHR.

Clinician Control & Safety Notice: AI Scribe does not make automated clinical decisions. All AI-generated notes remain draft suggestions until reviewed, edited, and explicitly approved by the authorized clinician.
